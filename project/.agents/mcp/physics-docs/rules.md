# Engineering physics

1. Before you write an equation of physics into a simulation (fluids, heat, acoustics, electricity, buoyancy, control), look it up with the `physics-docs` server: `search_docs`, then `read_doc` on one section. Never write a formula or a coefficient from memory.
2. It holds Wikipedia articles (naval architecture and submarines, underwater acoustics and sonar, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics and heat transfer, electricity and on-board power, control, numerical methods, nomograms), seven Wikibooks books, and the documented functions of fluids, ht, thermo, CoolProp, Cantera and PyNomo.
3. Search an equation or a law by its name, as `sonar equation`, `Colebrook` or `Rankine cycle`; a function of fluids or ht by its name, as `friction_factor`.
4. Give the units of every quantity, and say which source the equation comes from.
5. Read one section at a time. Never read the folder `.agents/mcp/physics-docs/library/` by hand.
