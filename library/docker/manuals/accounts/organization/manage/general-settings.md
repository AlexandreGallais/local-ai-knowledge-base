# Update organization information

Update the details that appear on your organization landing page.

## Update organization information

This information includes:

- Organization Name
- Company
- Location
- Website
- Gravatar email: To add an avatar to your Docker account, create a Gravatar account and upload an avatar. Next, add your Gravatar email to your Docker account settings. It may take some time for your avatar to update in Docker.

To edit this information:

1. Sign in to Docker Home and select your
   organization from the top-left account drop-down.
1. Select **Organization settings**.
1. Enter or update your organization's details, then select **Save**.

## Next steps

After configuring your organization information, you can:

- Configure single sign-on (SSO)
- Set up SCIM provisioning
- Manage domains
- Set up a company
