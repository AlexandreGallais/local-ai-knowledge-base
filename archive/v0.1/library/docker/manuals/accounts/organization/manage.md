# Manage your organization

Managing an organization is how you control who belongs to it and what each
member can use. Organization owners handle administration from
Docker Home.

## What organization management covers

Organization management covers two things: membership and the products your
members can use. Running both from one place keeps access aligned with your
team as it changes, so you can grow, reorganize, or offboard without
rebuilding access each time.

| To do this | Go here |
| --- | --- |
| Invite members, assign roles, and remove people who leave | Members |
| Group members and grant shared repository access | Teams |
| Add or remove seats on your Team or Business subscription | Seats |
| Assign add-on licenses for AI Governance, Docker Offload, and other products | Licenses |
| Control what members can use in each Docker product, and check usage | Products |
| Update the organization name, company, website, and avatar | Information |
| Deactivate the organization | Deactivation |
