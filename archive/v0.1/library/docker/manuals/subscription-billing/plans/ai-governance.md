# AI Governance plan

> **Tip:**
> To subscribe to AI Governance, contact sales.

AI Governance lets organization owners enforce policies for members across supported Docker AI products, like Docker Sandbox. When you add an AI Governance plan, you work with your Docker sales representative to determine the number of licenses needed, which are applied to your account.

## Usage

AI Governance lets organization owners enforce organization policies for license-holding members. Organization policies override a license-holding member's local policies.

You can assign AI Governance licenses to a team or to an individual organization member, even if they don't occupy a Docker Team or Docker Business seat. For best practice, review available licenses as you add new members since members without an AI Governance license can still use Docker AI products.

## Billing cycle

AI Governance plan pricing is based on the number of licenses purchased. To add or remove licenses, contact your Docker sales representative.
