# Docker Agentic Platform plans

> **Tip:**
> Docker Agentic Platform signups receive a one-time promotional
> credit toward cloud compute usage. To review your balance, see
> Credits.

Docker Agentic Platform is a
pay-as-you-go plan for running agent and tool workloads in isolated
sandboxes with Docker-managed cloud infrastructure. It also includes access to Docker Offload. You pay for the usage you accrue without a recurring
subscription fee.

To activate access for the Console, cloud sandbox CLI, Offload, or API and SDKs, follow
Signup and billing.

## Usage

> **Note:**
> Subscribe using your individual Docker account, including if you belong to
> an organization. The subscription is attached to your individual account.

Docker Agentic Platform requires bringing your own API keys for inference. Your inference provider
handles those costs.

After you subscribe, your invoice reflects metered, pay-as-you-go
compute usage. Docker meters compute usage in seconds, based on sandbox
runtime and the vCPU plus memory configuration you choose at setup.
Different sandbox configurations can produce different costs.

You can track usage and pricing from the
Usage & billing page in Agentic Platform.

To track usage for Docker Agentic Platform from Docker Home:

1. Sign in to Docker Home, then choose your
   account.
1. Go to **Billing** to view the Overview page, then go to **Active
   subscriptions**.
1. Select **Manage** next to **Docker Agentic Platform**.
1. View the estimated totals and usage for the current pay period.

## Billing cycle

Docker Agentic Platform is billed monthly on the day you subscribe.
Your invoice reflects compute usage accrued during the previous period.
For example:

- If you subscribed on September 10, you are billed on October 10.
- Your invoice from October 10 reflects accrued usage from September 10
  through October 9.
- Your invoice can take up to 24 hours after your billing date to
  appear.

Credits are applied to your costs at the end of the billing period.

## Cancel a plan

Canceling Docker Agentic Platform disables subscription renewal at the
start of the next plan period. Docker bills you for usage at the end of
the plan period.

1. Sign in to Docker Home and go to
   **Billing**.
1. From **Active subscriptions**, select **Manage** next to
   **Docker Agentic Platform**.
1. Select **Cancel subscription**.
1. Review your usage, then select **Cancel subscription** to confirm.

## Re-enable renewal

After you cancel, you can re-enable renewal at any time before the
current period ends. Your subscription then continues past the renewal
date.

1. Sign in to Docker Home and go to
   **Billing**.
1. From **Active subscriptions**, select **Manage** next to
   **Docker Agentic Platform**.
1. Select **Re-enable renewal**.
