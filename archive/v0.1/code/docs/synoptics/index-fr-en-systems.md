# French to English index, ship systems

Contents: 1. How to use · 2. Terms A to L · 3. Terms M to Z · Sources

## 1. How to use the French to English index

- Find the French term in the first column, lowercase. Use the English name of the second column in code, names and answers.
- Open the document of the third column, in `docs/synoptics/`, to read the card.
- The fluid circuit terms are in `index-fr-en-fluids.md`.
- French official terms of the ship rules come from the French maritime regulation; check each pair against the internal glossary of the team. [S1]
