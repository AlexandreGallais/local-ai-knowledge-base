# math index

One line per document. Open only the documents your task needs. The functions are in the kit: `kit/math/`.

- [Numbers in JavaScript: what a float does](number-basics.md): Binary floats, drift, order of operations, NaN, Infinity and -0, printing, Float32: the facts to know before any calculation.
- [Compare two computed numbers](compare-numbers.md): Never === on a computed value: absolute tolerance near zero, relative elsewhere, chosen from the data; sort with a comparator.
- [Round a number](rounding.md): floor, ceil, trunc, Math.round and its halves, half away from zero, half to even, to N decimals, to a step, to significant digits, toFixed.
- [Show a number](format-numbers.md): Intl.NumberFormat: decimals, rounding mode, percent, unit, negative zero, invisible separators of French output.
- [Read a number from a text](parse-numbers.md): Number, parseFloat and parseInt traps, a strict decimal reader, a decimal comma.
- [Integers and BigInt](integers-bigint.md): Safe integers up to 2^53 - 1, integer checks, BigInt rules and BigInt in JSON.
- [Ranges: modulo, clamp, interpolation, mapping, percent](range-operations.md): The floored modulo, clamp, lerp and its inverse, mapping a value from one range to another, even steps, percent.
- [Statistics of a list of values](statistics.md): Compensated sum, mean, median, variance and standard deviation, running statistics, moving average.
- [Smoothing and thresholds](filters-thresholds.md): Exponential smoothing and its factor from a time constant, hysteresis with two thresholds, deadband, alarm limit zones, NaN inputs.
- [Units and conversions](units.md): The unit in the name, conversion through one base unit, pressure, temperature and its difference, flow, duration, length, speed, rotation.
- [Angles](angles.md): Degrees and radians, normalizing, the shortest turn, interpolating an angle, atan2, results near zero and one.
- [Random values](random.md): Math.random and its limits, a random integer in a range, secure random, a seeded sequence for tests and demos.
- [Time and animation](time-animation.md): The two clocks, durations, progress of an animation, requestAnimationFrame, the fixed time step.
- [Test a calculation](test-calculations.md): Tolerance in tests, expected values from a source, properties when no value is known, edge cases.
