# geometry index

One line per document. Open only the documents your task needs. The functions are in the kit: `kit/geometry/`; call them through the geometry tools rather than computing by hand.

- [SVG geometry: vectors and distances](vectors.md): Add, subtract, scale, dot and cross products, length, normal vector, distance, midpoint, interpolation, with the kit functions.
- [SVG geometry: angles](angles.md): Degrees and radians, direction of a vector or between two points, signed angle between two vectors, in SVG coordinates.
- [SVG geometry: polar coordinates, circle, objects on a circle](circles.md): Polar coordinates, N points on a circle or an arc, chord and arc length, radius for a chord.
- [SVG geometry: svg arcs](svg-arcs.md): Numbers for SVG attributes, arc and sector paths, the endpoint and center forms of the SVG arc command, a point on an ellipse.
- [SVG geometry: transforms and matrices](matrices.md): The SVG matrix [a, b, c, d, e, f], translate, scale, rotate, skew, order of composition, inverse, decomposition.
- [SVG geometry: viewbox and screen coordinates](viewbox.md): The matrix of a viewBox and preserveAspectRatio, and a pointer position turned into user units.
- [SVG geometry: rectangles and bounding boxes](rectangles.md): Anchor points, bounding boxes, union, margin, containment, overlap, intersection, transformed box, edge point toward a target.
- [SVG geometry: intersections](intersections.md): Lines, segments and circles that cross, closest point and distance to a segment.
- [SVG geometry: bezier curves](bezier.md): Points, tangents, split, bounding box and length of Bezier curves, and an arc as cubic curves.
- [SVG geometry: path builders](path-builders.md): The d attribute of a polyline and of a rounded rectangle.
- [SVG geometry: alignment, distribution, grid, snapping](layout.md): Spread or stack items, grid cells, snap to a grid or to guides.
- [SVG geometry: polygons](polygons.md): Signed area, centroid, point inside, direction of the vertices and convexity of a polygon.
