# synoptics index

One line per document. Open only the documents your task needs.

- [Synoptic display rules](docs/synoptics/synoptic-rules.md): Rules to lay out a synoptic view and to show equipment states, alerts and flashing.
- [Symbol property naming](docs/synoptics/symbol-property-naming.md): Naming rules for the properties of a synoptic symbol and of its equipment model, with wrong and right examples.
- [Symbol component](docs/synoptics/symbol-component.md): How to code a reusable SVG symbol, with typed inputs, data attributes, CSS variables and one flash clock.
- [Fluid equipment property sets](docs/synoptics/fluid-common.md): The seven shared property sets of fluid equipment, their roles, and the rules that derive valve and drive states.
- [Fluid model laws and levels of detail](docs/synoptics/fluid-model-laws.md): Four laws and two relations that animate a fluid synoptic, and the three levels of model detail to choose from.
- [Hydraulic symbols catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-hydraulics.md): Fourteen hydraulic components with their ISO 1219-1 symbol, real working, states, property names and simple model.
- [Compressed air symbols catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-compressed-air.md): Six compressed air components with pressure levels, symbol, real working, states, property names and simple model.
- [Piping valves catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-piping-valves.md): Eight hand and self-acting piping valves with symbol, real working, states, property names and simple model.
- [Actuated valves and piping parts catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-actuated-valves.md): Motor, hydraulic, solenoid and control valves, three-way valve, strainers and manifold, with properties and simple model.
- [Ship fluid equipment catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-ship-equipment.md): Ten common ship fluid components, from pumps to steam trap, with symbol, real working, states, properties and model.
- [Ship fluid circuits and submarine tanks catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-ship-circuits.md): Five surface ship circuits and four public submarine ballast, trim and air cards, with properties and simple models.
- [Ventilation and air conditioning catalog](docs/synoptics/catalog-hvac.md): Seven ventilation and air conditioning components, fan to fan coil, with symbol, real working, states, properties and model.
- [French to English index, fluid circuits](docs/synoptics/index-fr-en-fluids.md): The English name of each French term of the fluid circuit catalogs, with the document that describes it.
